    Good to know

    ⚑ Sample policy – confirm before launch

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      Our in-house culinary team caters most events and can accommodate common dietary needs. External caterers may be approved for cultural or specialty cuisine, subject to proof of licensing and insurance and a kitchen-use fee.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      All alcohol is served by our licensed bar staff in line with local law. Guests must be of legal drinking age and may be asked for ID. Outside alcohol is not permitted unless agreed in writing; a corkage fee may apply.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      Amplified music on the Garden Terrace ends at 10:00 pm. Indoor music may continue until the end of your booking, subject to sound limits monitored by our team. Pyrotechnics and confetti cannons are not permitted.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      Setup access typically begins 3 hours before your start time and is included in most packages. Decorations must be free-standing. Nails, tape on walls and open flames (other than enclosed candles) are not permitted. Vendors must check in with your coordinator.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      Standard cleanup is included. Personal items, gifts and vendor equipment must be removed within 1 hour of your end time unless next-day collection is arranged. Excessive cleaning or damage may be charged against your security deposit.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      Cancellations must be made in writing. Deposits are generally non-refundable. Rescheduling to an available date within 12 months may be possible once without penalty. Full terms are set out in your booking agreement.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      All three spaces offer step-free access, with accessible restrooms and accessible parking bays near the entrance. The Grand Ballroom has a hearing loop and stage ramp. Service animals are welcome. Tell us about any access needs and we'll plan around them.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      We use the details you submit only to respond to your enquiry, arrange tours and manage your booking. We send marketing emails only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell your data. Contact us to access, correct or delete your information.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      An inquiry or quote does not reserve a date. Your booking is confirmed only when both parties sign the booking agreement and the deposit is received. Final guest numbers are due 14 days before the event. The hirer is responsible for guest conduct and any damage.

    • Sample policy – confirm before launch

      A deposit (typically 25–30% of the estimated total) secures your date. The remaining balance is due 30 days before your event. A refundable security deposit may be required. We accept bank transfer and major cards; card payments may carry a processing fee.
